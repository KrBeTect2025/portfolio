Headline project

lab-report-explainer

Lab report reader with checked results

Reads a lab report PDF, rebuilds the table of test results from the position of each word on the page, and marks every result low, normal or high using plain code, not an AI guess. A FastAPI service handles upload, lets a person review uncertain rows, and deletes the result after 24 hours.

Bugs I fixed, each with a test
  • A watermark leaking letters into numeric results
  • A "Method" column merging into the reference range
  • A symbol font being read as garbage characters

Known limits: tested on 4 public sample reports from 3 labs. Accuracy against hand-checked answers has not been measured yet.
