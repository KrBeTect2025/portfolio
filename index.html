lab-report-explainer

Reads a lab report PDF, rebuilds the table of test results from the position of each word on the page, and marks every result low, normal or high using plain code, not an AI guess. A FastAPI service handles upload, lets a person review uncertain rows, and deletes the result after 24 hours.
